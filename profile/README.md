## Build the company with us

Africa has exceptional talent and globally relevant problems. AERA provides the focused company-building infrastructure that helps founders turn both into enduring technology ventures.

We work as an aligned extension of the founding team—from discovery and validation through product, operations, go-to-market, capital readiness, and global scale. **You bring the insight. We build the company with you.**

## Our vision

A future in which world-defining technology companies are built from Africa—not despite where they begin, but because of the clarity, resilience, and market insight found here.

We believe founder potential is everywhere. Complete venture-building support is not. AERA closes that gap.

## What we do

- **Discover exceptional talent** before the market has fully noticed it.
- **Develop founders** into aligned, high-agency company builders.
- **Validate opportunities** with evidence from real customers and markets.
- **Build and ship** products that prove the core business thesis.
- **Create operating foundations** across technology, governance, and execution.
- **Build repeatable go-to-market systems** that turn product value into revenue.
- **Prepare companies for capital** before they enter the fundraising room.
- **Scale globally** with durable systems, leadership, and ambition.

## Our venture-building system

**Discover → Develop → Validate → Build → Test → Form → Enable → Go to market → Prepare for capital → Scale globally**

Ten stages. One aligned building team. From raw potential to a company built to endure.
